## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one small group of naive doodle shapes — the subject plus only the few props that explain what it is doing — redrawn front-on with no perspective or lighting, round and slightly lopsided, short and chubby, fur and foliage drawn only as a run of small round bumps along the outline. Every shape is wrapped in one thick, slightly wobbly black brush-ink line with rounded line ends and filled with a single flat opaque colour that sometimes misregisters past the line; people and animals have big heads and small bodies, two small black dot eyes and a tiny nose or one short mouth stroke. Everything stands on one bold black ground line drawn from the left edge to the right, the subject slightly to one side and the props staggered in height beside it, inside a wide shot with generous cream headroom above and nothing touching the fold. The field is a single flat light cream; black is used only for lines; the subject keeps the photograph's most present colour slightly desaturated, props take neighbouring muted tones, and only one small bright accent pops. When the photograph is a scene, draw only its most representative single object or fragment. The one short line of text is small, chubby hand lettering in the same thick ink, placed in the open space beside the subject like a comic-panel aside. Reject a full-scene copy, a filled background, thin uniform vector lines, colour fills with no ink outline, shapes floating off the ground line, realistic proportions, detailed faces, crowded props, shading, highlights, grain or distressing, glossy mascot cartoons, and neon colours.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-354/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
